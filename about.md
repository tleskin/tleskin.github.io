I'm Tom Leskin, a front-end engineer at Proofpoint in Seminole, Florida, where I build web apps with React and TypeScript.

I didn't start in software. I was a newspaper reporter first — I studied print journalism and reported for papers in Pennsylvania, including the Pottsville Republican-Herald, and did a journalism internship in Beijing. Then I went back to school for information science and technology, and in 2015 I moved across the country to Denver for a seven-month coding bootcamp at the Turing School of Software and Design. I've been building for the web ever since.

I write here about building things: the tools, the craft, and the fun of making something that didn't exist that morning and handing it to a real person by nightfall.

Outside of work I homebrew beer, collect Nintendo games, and tinker with local AI models on my Mac.
